Getting started

How do I start a group?

Open the app, name your group, enter the name you want your friends to see, and tap Create Group. You’re now the organizer.

How do my friends join?

Open the Group tab, tap Create invite link, then Send invite and text the link to your friends. When they tap it, the app opens and joins them to your group. They need the app installed first, and they need to be signed into iCloud.

Why does the app need iCloud?

Ready4Golf has no accounts or passwords. Your iCloud account is how the app knows who you are and how your group stays in sync between phones. If you’re not signed in, open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name at the top, and sign in.

The invite link doesn’t open the app.

Make sure Ready4Golf is installed on the phone that tapped the link, and that the phone is signed into iCloud. Try opening the link from Messages or Notes. If it still fails, email me and I’ll help.

Using the app

What do the numbers on the time blocks mean?

A small +2 on a block means two friends have already said yes to it, so you can see where your yes matters most.

What’s “players per tee time”?

The number of players that makes a window bookable (four for a foursome). It’s used for the “1 more needed” hints. It doesn’t stop anyone from locking in a smaller round.

Can I repeat my times every week?

Yes. Long-press a day for Repeat every Sat (or whichever day it is), or Always free this Sat to save it as your standing default.

Ready4Golf Pro

What does Pro include?

Free groups hold up to 4 players, and you can organize 1 group. Pro removes both limits. The organizer subscribes, and everyone in their group gets Pro. Members don’t pay.

How do I cancel?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Ready4Golf Pro and tap Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

I subscribed but my group doesn’t show Pro.

Open the paywall and tap Restore Subscription. Pro is applied to groups you organize, so make sure you’re looking at one you created, then close and reopen the app.

Can I get a refund?

Apple handles billing and refunds. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

How do I delete my data?

In the Group tab, swipe a group you created and tap Delete to remove it from iCloud. Swipe a group you joined and tap Leave to remove yourself. You can also remove everything in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Ready4Golf. Deleting the app on its own does not delete iCloud data. See the Privacy Policy for details.
