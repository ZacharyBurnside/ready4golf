Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

Ready4Golf (“the app”) is made by Zachary Burnside (“I”, “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses and where it goes.

The short version

  • The app has no accounts, passwords or servers of mine. Your information lives in your own iCloud account.
  • I can’t see the contents of your iCloud data.
  • There are no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics in the app.
  • I don’t sell your information.

What the app stores

  • The name you enter, which other members of your group see
  • Your group’s name, home course (if you add one) and settings, such as players per tee time
  • The times you mark as open, and your standing weekly defaults
  • The list of members in each group, and any tee time the group locks in
  • An identifier Apple provides for your iCloud account, so the app recognizes you across your devices
  • For a Pro group, the date the organizer’s subscription runs through

Where it’s stored

This information is stored with Apple’s iCloud (CloudKit) in your iCloud account. A group you create is stored in your account. When you invite friends, they can see and edit that group’s information, including member names and the times people marked. Groups you join are shared with you the same way. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is covered by Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Who can see it

Only the other members of groups you belong to. I do not receive copies of your group data, and the app does not use any server I operate.

Calendar

If you tap Add to calendar on a locked-in plan, iOS asks for permission to add events. The app only adds an event. It never reads your existing calendar events.

Purchases

Ready4Golf Pro is bought through the App Store, and Apple processes the payment. The app learns only whether a subscription is active and when it ends. I never receive your payment details.

Analytics and crash data

The app does not include third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. Apple may share with me aggregated, anonymous App Store statistics and crash reports from people who have chosen to share them with developers.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • In the Group tab, swipe a group you created and tap Delete to remove it and its data from iCloud.
  • Swipe a group you joined and tap Leave to remove your membership.
  • You can also remove the app’s data in Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Storage → Ready4Golf.
  • Deleting the app by itself does not delete data in iCloud.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, I’ll update this page and the date above.
